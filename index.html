Brand guidelines (Working draft)

How we look, sound and present ourselves

This overview of our brand identity is designed to help you capture the Stationers’ look, feel and tone of voice in a consistent and professional way. We should aim to connect to our audience, be direct and hold attention. To achieve this our communications should be clear, professional and organised.

What not to do

These apply to every mark.

  • Don’t stretch, squash, rotate or crop the logo.
  • Don’t change its colours or use colours outside the supplied versions.
  • Don’t move, resize or separate the eagle and wordmark, or create new arrangements.
  • Don’t retype the wordmark in another font or redraw any part of the logo.
  • Don’t add shadows, outlines, glows, gradients or other effects.
  • Don’t place the logo on busy backgrounds or colours with weak contrast.
  • Don’t copy the logo from websites, screenshots or old documents. Download it from this page.

02 · Typefaces

The Company uses three typefaces: Larken for headings, Hanken Grotesk for body copy and Manrope for buttons and labels on screen. Print adds Tiempos Text for body copy.

Headings · Larken

Use Larken for display text and headings, on screen and in print. Keep the number of heading levels limited so the hierarchy stays clear.

Larken Regular, Regular Italic, Bold and Bold Italic

Body copy · Hanken Grotesk

Use Hanken Grotesk for paragraphs and longer reading text on screen, and for secondary headings in print.

05 · Tone of voice

Our tone should encourage the audience to engage with the content and the brand, to learn more about the Company, and start building a loyal, lasting relationship with us. This comes through on our website, social media and in any other written communication.

Be inclusive

  • "Our members" instead of "members"
  • "Our Company" instead of "the Company"
  • "We" and "us" instead of "staff"
  • "Our network" instead of "The network"

Use an active voice

  • "Be part of . . ."
  • "In our community . . ."
  • "Socialise with/meet fellow Stationers at . . ."
  • "Together we can . . ."

Make it personal

  • "Come and join us", "meet us", instead of "apply" or "sign up"
  • "Get in touch", instead of "call"
  • "Join us", instead of "You are invited"
  • "Our family of 1000 members"

Connect to the outside world

  • "A cosmopolitan hub for major industries"
  • "Excellent transport links to all that London offers"
  • "Where motivated people from around the UK and beyond come together"
