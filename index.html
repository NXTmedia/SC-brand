Brand guidelines (Working draft)

How we look, sound and present ourselves

This overview of our brand identity is designed to help you capture the Stationers’ look, feel and tone of voice in a consistent and professional way. We should aim to connect to our audience, be direct and hold attention. To achieve this our communications should be clear, professional and organised.

07 · Tone of voice

Our tone should encourage the audience to engage with the content and the brand, to learn more about the Company, and start building a loyal, lasting relationship with us. This comes through on our website, social media and in any other written communication.

Be inclusive

  • "Our members" instead of "members"
  • "Our Company" instead of "the Company"
  • "We" and "us" instead of "staff"
  • "Our network" instead of "The network"

Use an active voice

  • "Be part of . . ."
  • "In our community . . ."
  • "Socialise with/meet fellow Stationers at . . ."
  • "Together we can . . ."

Make it personal

  • "Come and join us", "meet us", instead of "apply" or "sign up"
  • "Get in touch", instead of "call"
  • "Join us", instead of "You are invited"
  • "Our family of 1000 members"

Connect to the outside world

  • "A cosmopolitan hub for major industries"
  • "Excellent transport links to all that London offers"
  • "Where motivated people from around the UK and beyond come together"
